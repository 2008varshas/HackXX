🚨 RESQORA – Intelligent Emergency Response & Disaster Management Platform

Tagline: One Report. One Network. Faster Response.

🌍 About the Project

RESQORA is an intelligent, centralized Emergency Response and Disaster Management Platform designed to improve emergency reporting, incident prioritization, rescue coordination, and resource management.

It connects citizens, administrators, volunteers, and emergency response teams through a unified digital platform to support faster and more organized emergency response.

🎯 Problem Statement

During emergencies and natural disasters, delayed communication, poor coordination, inefficient resource allocation, and lack of real-time information can affect rescue operations. RESQORA addresses these challenges through an integrated emergency management system.

✨ ROUND 1 – Core Features

User Authentication: Registration, login, and role-based dashboards.

Emergency Incident Reporting: Report fire accidents, road accidents, medical emergencies, floods, building collapses, and other incidents.

Severity Classification: Categorizes incidents as Low, Medium, High, and Critical.

Community Verification: Supports verification of reported emergencies.

Offline Reporting: Saves emergency reports locally when internet connectivity is unavailable and synchronizes them when connectivity returns.

Multilingual Assistance: Supports English, Tamil, and Hindi.

Emergency Resource Finder: Helps locate hospitals, ambulances, fire stations, police stations, shelters, and relief centres.

Emergency Map & Heatmap: Visualizes emergency locations.

Volunteer Management: Supports volunteer registration and coordination.

AI-Assisted Analysis: Assists with incident category suggestions, severity recommendations, and summaries.

Notifications & Tracking: Provides incident updates and tracking.

Safety Guidelines: Provides emergency-related safety instructions.

🚀 ROUND 2 – Advanced Features

1. Intelligent Incident Prioritization

Automatically calculates incident priority scores.

Considers severity, number of people affected, waiting time, and incident category.

Ranks incidents based on priority.

Allows administrators to override priority with a recorded reason.

Maintains severity and priority as separate classifications.

2. Emergency Response Team Management

Create and manage emergency response teams.

Organize teams into Medical, Fire & Rescue, Flood Response, Disaster Management, and General Rescue categories.

Assign team leaders and members.

Maintain team locations.

Track availability as Available, Busy, or Unavailable.

3. Location-Based Nearest Team Assignment

Identifies suitable teams based on emergency category.

Filters teams according to availability.

Calculates geographical distance using the Haversine formula.

Recommends the nearest suitable available team.

Supports manual team assignment and administrator override.

Identifies when no suitable team is available.

4. Emergency Assignment & Incident Tracking

Assign incidents to response teams.

Track the complete incident lifecycle:
Reported → Assigned → In Progress → Resolved

Maintain assignment history.

Record incident status updates.

Automatically update team availability after assignment and resolution.

5. Advanced Emergency Map Visualization

Displays incident and response team locations.

Shows nearby emergency resources.

Displays geographical distances between incidents and teams.

Supports road route visualization when a routing service is configured.

Distinguishes straight-line distance from actual road distance.

6. Advanced Analytics Dashboard

Total emergency incidents.

Incidents by category and severity.

Incident status distribution.

Priority-based incident statistics.

Available and busy response teams.

Team assignment statistics.

Emergency response trends.

Geographical distance analysis.

7. In-App Notifications

Team assignment notifications.

Emergency status change notifications.

Incident updates for relevant users.

Notifications for administrators and response teams.

🔄 How RESQORA Works

Citizens report emergencies through the platform.

The system records incident details and location.

Incidents are classified and prioritized.

The platform recommends suitable nearby response teams.

Administrators confirm or modify team assignments.

Response teams handle emergencies and update their progress.

Administrators monitor incidents through maps and analytics.

Resolved incidents are recorded, and team availability is updated.
